## Purpose

`06-risk-list.md` contains the narrative rationale, treatment classes, first-slice
gates and capability gates. This register makes every `R-*` record structurally
complete against the method required by #115.

The two files form one Risk List deliverable. A risk is not considered reviewed
unless its narrative entry and this register agree.

Severity and priority remain hypotheses until measured evidence exists. The
`Evidence available` column records accepted decisions or current repository
facts, not proof that the risk is mitigated.

## Completeness rule

For review purposes, each `R-*` record consists of:

1. the summary/priority entry in `06-risk-list.md`;
2. the narrative risk section in `06-risk-list.md` where present; and
3. the structurally complete row in this register.

A future risk-state update must keep these surfaces consistent. If a risk gains
measured probability, quantitative threshold or implementation control, the
source of that evidence must be linked rather than silently replacing the
Inception hypothesis.
