# Project Genesis

Project Genesis was Raiatea's **Inception** phase under a lean, risk-driven
Unified Process. It is complete in the accepted target state of the final
[`Inception Review`](inception/08-inception-review.md); `main` becomes
authoritative when PR #122 is merged.

The resulting phase decision is a bounded **GO to Elaboration for P0 risk
reduction and architecture**. It is not a software release, does not claim P0 is
implemented and does not promote the candidate first product slice to `planned`
work.

## What Project Genesis produced

Genesis established a reviewed route from founding ideas to a canonical
Inception package:

- [`00-why-raiatea.md`](inception/00-why-raiatea.md) — problem, first person,
  hypotheses, P0 priority and kill criteria;
- [`01-manifesto.md`](inception/01-manifesto.md) — durable human and product
  commitments;
- [`02-vision.md`](inception/02-vision.md) — long-term direction, product
  hierarchy and P0-P7 destination capabilities;
- [`03-system-context.md`](inception/03-system-context.md) — Raiatea, Alfred,
  Durex, TheBitLab and external-system boundaries;
- [`04-product-map.md`](inception/04-product-map.md) — Universal Document &
  Asset Library, Research & Learning Workspace and shared capability map;
- [`05-use-case-model.md`](inception/05-use-case-model.md) — significant user
  goals, alternatives, failures and invariants;
- [`06-risk-list.md`](inception/06-risk-list.md) and
  [`06-risk-register.md`](inception/06-risk-register.md) — 23 risks, first-slice
  planning gates G-01 through G-07, feature gates and kill/narrowing criteria;
- [`07-glossary.md`](inception/07-glossary.md) and
  [`07-terminology-compatibility.md`](inception/07-terminology-compatibility.md)
  — initial ubiquitous language and compatibility with pre-Glossary wording;
- [`08-inception-review.md`](inception/08-inception-review.md) — final phase gate
  and bounded Elaboration decision.

The canonical index, document lifecycle and assertion-status vocabulary remain
in [`inception/README.md`](inception/README.md).

## What remains deliberately open

Completing Genesis does not answer questions that require Elaboration evidence.
In particular it does not choose:

- P0 parsers, OCR engines, VLMs or other providers;
- database, search, vector or graph technology;
- final P0 technical schema/API representation;
- quantitative first-slice thresholds;
- cross-platform Alfred implementation details;
- Durex Job/Run reuse;
- automatic organization design;
- translation/layout reconstruction strategy;
- a formal Work/Expression/Manifestation/Item model;
- P1-P7 implementation order;
- the outcome of the independent pedagogical pilot.

The candidate PDF/EPUB first slice remains a **working hypothesis** until the
accepted G-01..G-07 evidence gates are satisfied and a separate roadmap decision
promotes it.

## Next phase

Raiatea now enters **Elaboration — P0 risk reduction and architecture**.

The active platform roadmap is [#106](https://github.com/kinderp/raiatea/issues/106).
The immediate evidence-driven work is limited to:

1. source taxonomy, Processing Rights and threat/data-flow boundary;
2. current technology survey and build/buy/reuse matrix;
3. rights-safe benchmark corpus/fixture design;
4. source-class benchmark contract and baseline measurement plan;
5. provider-neutral P0 contract exploration;
6. Alfred reconciliation/integration evidence;
7. evidence packages for G-01 through G-07.

Automatic organization, natural-language search, translation/layout,
multi-output DAG execution, Durex integration, physical-holding linking,
TheBitLab projection and P1-P7 remain behind their accepted feature/evidence
gates.

The supervised pedagogical pilot remains a separate evidence stream and is not
validated by the phase transition.

## Exploratory notes

The numbered files in this directory remain **historical exploratory sources**.
They preserve the reasoning, alternatives and long-term research behind Raiatea
and are not automatically current decisions.

The notes from `00-understanding.md` through
`20-pedagogical-visual-standard.md` remain at their current paths so historical
links and provenance are preserved. Their complete linked legend is available
in the [Genesis audit](inception/00-genesis-audit-and-traceability.md#21-legenda-delle-fonti-genesis).

Future work should cite the accepted canonical Inception package for current
product/architecture guidance and return to these exploratory notes when their
historical reasoning is relevant.
