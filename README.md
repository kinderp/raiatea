# Raiatea

*A private research project for navigating evolving knowledge with provenance, context, and human judgment.*

Raiatea has completed **Project Genesis / Inception** and is entering a bounded
**Elaboration** phase focused on reducing the risks of the P0 Source Ingestion &
Extraction foundation.

This phase transition does **not** mean that the Universal Document & Asset
Library or P0 already exists as a general implementation. It also does not
promote the candidate PDF/EPUB first slice to `planned` work by itself.

## Current phase

**Elaboration — P0 risk reduction and architecture.**

The active platform objective is to produce evidence for the accepted P0
roadmap rather than to expand the vision. Current authorized work includes:

- source taxonomy, Processing Rights and bounded threat/data-flow analysis;
- current technology survey and build/buy/reuse comparison;
- rights-safe benchmark fixtures and source-class metrics;
- provider-neutral P0 contract exploration using the canonical Glossary;
- Alfred reconciliation/integration evidence;
- evidence required by first-slice planning gates G-01 through G-07.

The candidate first product slice remains a **working hypothesis** until those
gates have sufficient evidence and a separate roadmap decision promotes it.
Automatic organization, natural-language search, translation/layout
reconstruction, multi-output DAG execution, Durex integration, physical-holding
linking, TheBitLab projection and P1-P7 remain behind their accepted evidence or
feature gates.

The supervised pedagogical pilot continues as an **independent evidence stream**.
Entering P0 Elaboration is not evidence that Raiatea improves learning outcomes.

See [`genesis/inception/08-inception-review.md`](genesis/inception/08-inception-review.md)
for the phase decision and [issue #106](https://github.com/kinderp/raiatea/issues/106)
for the active P0 platform roadmap.
